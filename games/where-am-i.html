About the map data

Silhouettes are generated from Natural Earth 1:50m country boundaries. Dependent territories and Antarctica are excluded. Taiwan follows Natural Earth’s country classification; Israel and Palestine are included as named country records. Distant overseas components are omitted so the main outline stays legible.
